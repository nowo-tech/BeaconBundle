  Beacon Bundle installed.

  Set BEACON_DSN to your Symfony Beacon instance, e.g.:
    BEACON_DSN=https://PUBLIC_KEY@beacon.example.com:9444/1

  Empty BEACON_DSN disables reporting.

  Verify end to end with real failures:
    php bin/console nowo:beacon:self-test captured-exception
